Nitos_OS wallpaper assets

Place your original Nitos_OS wallpaper artwork in this directory.

Recommended:
  nitos-os-wallpaper.png

For best results, use a 16:9 image at 1920x1080 or higher.

The image must be copied into the Cubic custom-root and configured
as the Plasma wallpaper separately. Creating this directory alone
does not change the active desktop wallpaper.
